The Problem
Drug development is too slow, too expensive, and too manual
The pharmaceutical industry spends billions on regulatory processes that haven't fundamentally changed in decades.
  • ⏱️

    Painfully slow timelines

    An Investigator's Brochure takes 3–6 months to write using traditional approaches. Regulatory bottlenecks delay trial starts by quarters.

  • 💰

    Spiraling costs

    Traditional CROs charge premium rates for labor-intensive manual work. Small biotechs burn capital waiting for deliverables.

  • 📋

    Inconsistent quality

    Document quality varies wildly between writers. Junior staff handle senior-level work. Errors compound through each revision cycle.

  • 🔍

    Missing intelligence

    Most regulatory teams work in silos, without systematic access to competitive intelligence, site performance data, or market insights.

Our Approach
We don't replace expertise with AI.
We amplify it.

Acriba is a consulting and operating firm that uses the latest advancements in AI, coupled with subject-matter experts across every discipline of drug development. The result: faster timelines, lower costs, and higher quality — without compromise.
