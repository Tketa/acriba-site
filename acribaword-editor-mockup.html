IB 5 · EFFECTS IN HUMANS · 5.2 PHARMACODYNAMICS
5.2 Pharmacodynamics

In the Phase Ib dose-escalation study (AB-104-101), ABX-104 administered at 120 mg QD produced a dose-dependent reduction in serum biomarker X of 58% (90% CI: 44–69) versus baseline at Week 8.

Pharmacodynamic responses in the 60 mg and 90 mg cohorts were consistent with the exposure–response model fitted to Phase I data (Section 5.1.3), supporting the rationale for the recommended Phase II dose.

Two SAEs were reported in the 120 mg cohort and were classified as treatment-related adverse events; both resolved without sequelae.

Across all evaluable subjects, the onset of pharmacodynamic effect was observed within 72 hours of first dose
